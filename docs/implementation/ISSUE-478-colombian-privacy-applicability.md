# Issue #478 — Colombian Privacy Review for SaludVital

- **Issue:** [#478 — Review privacy rules for SaludVital data use](https://github.com/personalProjectsDeltaG8109/saludvital/issues/478)
- **Branch:** `docs/478-data-protection-review`
- **Review date:** 2026-11-16

## Purpose and limits

This document compares verified repository behavior and owner-confirmed demonstration practices with official Colombian sources. It is a planning review, not legal advice, an audit of a live deployment, or a statement that SaludVital is compliant. A qualified Colombian privacy and health-sector lawyer must confirm legal conclusions before this project is used to provide real care.

The review did not read `.env`, contact a provider, inspect a hosting account, or access personal records. Configuration was reviewed through `.env.example` and the checked-in provider documentation only.

## Facts found

- SaludVital is an individual SENA ADSO graduation and demonstration project. The public terms describe it as an educational demonstration, not a clinic or medical-care service.
- The owner-confirmed demonstration rules recorded in the privacy notice are: seed examples are synthetic; a tester may use their own identity and contact details in controlled tests; clinical examples must remain fictional; and other people's information must not be entered.
- The account and patient models support names, email, phone, date of birth, identity-document fields, address, notification destinations, blood type, allergies, and profile-photo references. Consultation records support symptoms, patient-reported feelings, vital signs, clinical notes, recommendations, diagnoses, and medical orders.
- Registration requires a consent checkbox. Since Issue #468, the application stores a linked consent scope, privacy-notice version, and acceptance time. The stored evidence does not include the submitted clinical values, IP address, or device details.
- Unverified patient registrations without protected related records are removed after the configured waiting period, which defaults to 30 minutes. Verified accounts and their appointment, consultation, notification, and audit history do not have a general automatic deletion period in the inspected cleanup services.
- WhatsApp message content has a configurable retention period capped at 30 days. The cleanup clears content but keeps conversation and message metadata.
- Profile photos are delivered through an authenticated route and new uploads are re-encoded to remove image metadata. The documented legacy-photo migration has not been run against existing project data.
- The application contains adapters for Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, Resend, Inalambria Express, and Railway deployment configuration. Their presence in the repository does not prove which services or regions are active in a specific deployment. The owner has reported a successful Inalambria test, but the provider contract, processing locations, and account retention terms are not in this repository.
- The application supports pediatric appointment eligibility for patients aged 0–17 on the appointment date. A search of the patient profile and registration code found no explicit parent or legal-representative relationship field or consent workflow.
- The privacy page explains data rights and provides a contact email, but the repository does not contain a documented internal process for assigning, tracking, and responding to requests or privacy incidents.

## Owner decisions and missing evidence

These points are not answerable from checked-in code. Record them before claiming the review is final for a live deployment:

1. Is the running instance only a private graduation demonstration, or is any real clinic or professional using it to provide care?
2. Which person or legal entity determines the purposes and means of the live processing, and does that operator have a public, company, or non-profit legal form?
3. Does any test or deployment contain data about someone other than the owner, real health details, or a real person under 18? The documented demo rule says not to add these.
4. Which provider accounts and hosting regions actually receive data, and what do their current contracts say about storage, subprocessors, support access, and deletion?
5. If the system will be used for actual care, who is responsible for clinical-history custody and retention, and what health-sector rules govern the specific records?
6. Who will handle data-access/correction/deletion requests and security incidents, and what written operating procedure will they follow?
7. Based on the operator's legal form and assets, is RNBD registration required?

## Outcome and next steps

- The current public pages already describe the educational demonstration and the code-backed retention behavior. This review found no evidence-based wording change that should be made before the responsible party and deployment facts above are confirmed.
- The repository shows real technical safeguards, including consent evidence, private profile-photo delivery, limited WhatsApp message retention, and an unverified-registration cleanup. These controls do not by themselves establish legal sufficiency.
- No source code, database schema, or deployment setting was changed for this review. Do not describe the project as legally certified or fully compliant based on this document.
- Obtain qualified Colombian privacy and, if applicable, health-sector legal review before using real patient/child health data or operating as a care service. Update the public policy only after that review and the owner decisions are recorded.

## Official sources reviewed

- [Law 1581 of 2012 — Función Pública](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=49981): application, data roles, principles, sensitive health data, minors, authorization, rights, security, and international transfers.
- [Decree 1074 of 2015 — Función Pública](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=76608): regulations for personal-data processing, controller/processor relationships, and RNBD.
- [Decree 1377 of 2013 — Función Pública](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=53646): authorization and safeguards for minors' personal data.
- [SIC guide to personal data protection (2023)](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/sites/default/files/boletin-juridico/boletin/docs/Guia%20de%20datos%202023.pdf): general guidance on roles, duties, and accountability.
- [SIC guidance on who must register in the RNBD](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/publicaciones/boletin-juridico/concepto/cuales-personas-estan-obligadas-realizar-el-registro-de-bases-de-datos-personales-en-el-rnbd): legal-form and asset-threshold conditions, and duties that remain for non-registrants.
- [SIC Circular External 003 of 2025](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/transparencia/normativa/circular-externa-no-003-del-19-de-diciembre-de-2025): model clauses for international personal-data transfers.
- [SIC security-incident management guide](https://sedeelectronica.sic.gov.co/noticias/superindustria-expide-guia-para-la-gestion-de-incidentes-de-seguridad-en-el-tratamiento-de-datos-personales): official incident-management guidance.
- [Resolution 839 of 2017 — Ministry of Health](https://minsalud.gov.co/Normatividad_Nuevo/Resolucion%20No%20839%20de%202017.pdf): modifies clinical-history retention and custody rules when they apply to a health provider.
